METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
====================
Trace variants are sequences of payment timing categories, not complete loan application workflows.
Events are ordered by relative payment day, installment number, and installment version.
The secondary sort fields make ordering reproducible, but do not establish the true order of events with identical payment days.
